The 2024 Toyota Corolla Hybrid SE AWD is a sporty and fuel-efficient compact sedan that combines modern styling, advanced hybrid technology, everyday comfort, and confident all-weather capability. With its athletic SE design and Toyota's reputation for reliability, the Corolla Hybrid is an excellent choice for commuting and daily driving. Powered by a 1.8L 4-cylinder engine paired with Toyota's hybrid electric system, the Corolla delivers a combined 138 horsepower through an electronically controlled continuously variable transmission (eCVT). Its Electronic On-Demand All-Wheel Drive system provides additional traction when needed, while maintaining impressive fuel efficiency without requiring external charging. The SE trim features sport-inspired styling, alloy wheels, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, automatic climate control, sport fabric seating, and convenient 60/40 split-folding rear seats. Technology highlights include an 8-inch Toyota Multimedia touchscreen with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, Bluetooth® connectivity, USB-C ports, steering wheel-mounted audio controls, and a rearview camera. Toyota Safety Sense™ 3.0 includes Pre-Collision System with Pedestrian Detection, Full-Speed Range Dynamic Radar Cruise Control, Lane Departure Alert with Steering Assist, Lane Tracing Assist, Road Sign Assist, and Automatic High Beams. The 2024 Toyota Corolla Hybrid SE AWD delivers an excellent combination of outstanding fuel efficiency, sporty styling, advanced safety, and year-round driving confidence, making it a practical and well-equipped hybrid sedan.
