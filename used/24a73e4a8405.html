The 2022 Hyundai Venue Preferred Two-Tone is a stylish and fuel-efficient subcompact SUV that offers exceptional value, modern technology, and everyday practicality. With its eye-catching two-tone exterior, compact size, and elevated driving position, the Venue is perfect for city driving while providing the versatility and comfort of an SUV. Powered by a reliable 1.6L Smartstream 4-cylinder engine producing 121 horsepower and paired with Hyundai's Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT), the Venue delivers smooth performance, responsive handling, and impressive fuel economy for both urban commuting and highway travel. The distinctive exterior features exclusive two-tone paint with a contrasting roof, 15-inch alloy wheels, LED daytime running lights, roof rails, and Hyundai's bold cascading grille, giving the Venue a youthful and modern appearance. Inside, the Preferred trim offers a comfortable and well-equipped cabin with heated front seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, automatic climate control, premium cloth seating, proximity key with push-button start, and versatile 60/40 split-folding rear seats for added cargo flexibility. Technology highlights include an 8-inch touchscreen infotainment system with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto, Bluetooth® connectivity, multiple USB ports, SiriusXM® capability, and a rearview camera, keeping you connected and entertained wherever you go. Hyundai SmartSense safety features include Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist with Pedestrian Detection, Lane Keeping Assist, Driver Attention Warning, High Beam Assist, Rear Occupant Alert, Electronic Stability Control, and multiple airbags, providing added confidence on every drive. The 2022 Hyundai Venue Preferred Two-Tone is an excellent choice for drivers seeking a stylish, economical, and feature-packed compact SUV that delivers outstanding value, advanced safety, and everyday versatility.
