The 2025 Honda HR-V Sport AWD is a stylish and versatile compact SUV that combines everyday practicality, confident all-weather capability, and Hondas reputation for reliability. With its sporty design, comfortable interior, and advanced safety technology, the HR-V Sport is an excellent choice for commuters, small families, and active lifestyles. Powered by a 2.0L 4-cylinder engine producing 158 horsepower and paired with a smooth Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT), the HR-V delivers refined performance and impressive fuel efficiency. Hondas Real Time AWD system provides enhanced traction and stability in rain, snow, and changing road conditions. The Sport trim features 17-inch alloy wheels, LED headlights, heated front seats, a heated leather-wrapped steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, remote engine start, proximity key with push-button start, and versatile 60/40 split-folding rear seats for added cargo flexibility. Technology highlights include a touchscreen infotainment system with Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto, Bluetooth® connectivity, USB ports, and a multi-angle rearview camera, keeping you connected and confident on every journey. Honda Sensing® safety technologies include Collision Mitigation Braking System, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping Assist System, Road Departure Mitigation, Traffic Sign Recognition, and Forward Collision Warning. Blind Spot Information and Rear Cross Traffic Monitor provide additional awareness. The 2025 Honda HR-V Sport AWD offers an excellent combination of reliability, advanced safety, modern technology, and all-weather versatility, making it a smart choice for drivers seeking a practical and well-equipped compact SUV.
