The 2024 Hyundai Tucson Hybrid Ultimate AWD is a premium compact SUV that combines impressive hybrid fuel efficiency, sophisticated styling, luxury-level comfort, and advanced technology. With its spacious interior and versatile cargo area, the Tucson Hybrid is an excellent choice for families, commuting, and everyday adventures. Powered by a 1.6L turbocharged 4-cylinder engine paired with an electric motor, the Tucson Hybrid delivers a combined 226 horsepower through a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission. Hyundai’s HTRAC™ All-Wheel Drive system provides confident traction and stability in changing road and weather conditions. The Ultimate trim offers premium features including leather seating surfaces, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, a heated steering wheel, power-adjustable front seats, dual-zone automatic climate control, a panoramic sunroof, and a hands-free smart power liftgate. Technology highlights include a 10.25-inch touchscreen with navigation, a 10.25-inch digital instrument cluster, Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, Bose® premium audio, wireless phone charging, Hyundai Bluelink®, Head-Up Display, Surround View Monitor, and Blind-Spot View Monitor. Hyundai SmartSense™ safety technologies include Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Blind-Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist, Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist, Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Lane Keeping Assist, Lane Following Assist, and Highway Driving Assist. The 2024 Hyundai Tucson Hybrid Ultimate AWD delivers an exceptional combination of hybrid efficiency, premium comfort, advanced safety, and year-round versatility, making it an outstanding choice for drivers seeking a fully equipped hybrid SUV.
