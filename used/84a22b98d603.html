The 2019 Hyundai Elantra Luxury is a refined and well-equipped compact sedan that combines excellent fuel efficiency, premium comfort, and advanced safety technology. With its stylish design and spacious interior, the Elantra Luxury is an excellent choice for commuters, families, and drivers looking for an upscale yet affordable daily vehicle. Powered by a 2.0L 4-cylinder engine producing 147 horsepower and paired with a smooth 6-speed automatic transmission, the Elantra delivers responsive performance and impressive efficiency for both city and highway driving. The Luxury trim features leather seating surfaces, heated front and rear seats, a heated steering wheel, power-adjustable drivers seat, dual-zone automatic climate control, power sunroof, proximity key with push-button start, and generous passenger and trunk space. Technology highlights include a touchscreen infotainment system with built-in navigation, Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto, Bluetooth® connectivity, wireless phone charging, Infinity® premium audio, USB connectivity, and a rearview camera. Advanced safety features include Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Blind-Spot Collision Warning, Rear Cross-Traffic Collision Warning, Lane Keeping Assist, Driver Attention Warning, and Adaptive Cruise Control for added confidence behind the wheel. The 2019 Hyundai Elantra Luxury delivers an excellent combination of premium features, fuel efficiency, advanced technology, and everyday practicality, making it a smart choice for drivers seeking exceptional value in a well-equipped compact sedan.
