The 2023 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport Highline 4MOTION is a premium and spacious midsize SUV that combines bold styling, refined comfort, and confident all-weather capability. With its sporty two-row design and generous cargo space, its an excellent choice for drivers seeking versatility without sacrificing luxury. Powered by a 3.6L V6 engine producing 276 horsepower and paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, the Atlas Cross Sport delivers smooth, confident performance. Volkswagens 4MOTION® All-Wheel Drive system provides enhanced traction and stability in rain, snow, and changing road conditions. The Highline trim offers a premium interior featuring leather seating surfaces, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, a heated steering wheel, driver memory, three-zone automatic climate control, panoramic sunroof, and a hands-free power liftgate. Technology highlights include a touchscreen infotainment system with navigation, Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto, Volkswagen Digital Cockpit, wireless phone charging, Fender® premium audio, and a 360-degree Area View camera system. Advanced driver-assistance features include Adaptive Cruise Control, Front Assist with Autonomous Emergency Braking, Blind Spot Monitor, Rear Traffic Alert, Lane Assist, and parking assistance for added confidence. The 2023 Volkswagen Atlas Cross Sport Highline 4MOTION delivers an impressive combination of V6 performance, premium comfort, advanced technology, and all-weather versatility, making it an outstanding choice for drivers seeking a sophisticated and well-equipped midsize SUV.
