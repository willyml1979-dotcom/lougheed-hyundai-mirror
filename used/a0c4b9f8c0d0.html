The 2016 Subaru Forester 2.5i Limited AWD is a practical and versatile compact SUV that combines dependable performance, premium comfort, excellent visibility, and confident all-weather capability. With its spacious interior, generous cargo capacity, and impressive ground clearance, the Forester is an excellent choice for families, commuting, and outdoor adventures. Powered by a 2.5L SUBARU BOXER® 4-cylinder engine producing 170 horsepower and 174 lb-ft of torque, paired with a Lineartronic® Continuously Variable Transmission (CVT), the Forester delivers smooth and efficient everyday performance. Subaru’s Symmetrical All-Wheel Drive system provides excellent traction and stability in rain, snow, and changing road conditions. The Limited trim offers premium comfort and convenience features, including leather-trimmed seating, heated front seats, a power-adjustable driver’s seat, automatic climate control, a panoramic power moonroof, keyless access with push-button start, and a power rear liftgate. Technology highlights include a touchscreen infotainment system, Bluetooth® connectivity, USB inputs, steering wheel-mounted audio controls, and a rearview camera. Safety features include Vehicle Dynamics Control, Traction Control, anti-lock brakes, multiple airbags, and Subaru’s reinforced body structure. The 2016 Subaru Forester 2.5i Limited AWD delivers an excellent combination of comfort, practicality, spacious versatility, and year-round driving confidence, making it a well-rounded compact SUV.
