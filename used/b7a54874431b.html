The 2022 Ford Escape SE AWD is a versatile and efficient compact SUV that combines comfortable everyday driving, modern technology, practical cargo space, and confident all-weather capability. Its compact dimensions make it easy to maneuver around the city while still providing the flexibility expected from an SUV. Powered by a 1.5L EcoBoost® turbocharged 3-cylinder engine producing 181 horsepower and 190 lb-ft of torque, paired with an 8-speed automatic transmission, the Escape delivers responsive performance and excellent everyday efficiency. Intelligent All-Wheel Drive provides added traction and confidence in rain, snow, and changing road conditions. Inside, the Escape SE offers comfortable seating for five, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, dual-zone automatic climate control, remote start, keyless entry, and flexible 60/40 split-folding rear seats for additional cargo versatility. Technology features include Ford SYNC® 3 with an 8-inch touchscreen, Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto, Bluetooth® connectivity, USB ports, steering wheel-mounted controls, and a rearview camera. Ford Co-Pilot360 driver-assistance technologies include Pre-Collision Assist with Automatic Emergency Braking, Blind Spot Information System with Cross-Traffic Alert, Lane-Keeping System, Auto High-Beam Headlamps, and a rearview camera. The 2022 Ford Escape SE AWD offers an excellent combination of turbocharged efficiency, useful technology, safety, and year-round versatility in a practical compact SUV.
