The 2021 Hyundai Elantra Ultimate is a premium and well-equipped compact sedan that combines bold styling, excellent fuel efficiency, advanced technology, and upscale comfort. As the top trim in the Elantra lineup, the Ultimate offers an impressive selection of features while remaining practical and economical for everyday driving. Powered by a 2.0L Smartstream 4-cylinder engine producing 147 horsepower and paired with Hyundais Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT), the Elantra delivers smooth performance and impressive fuel economy for both city commuting and highway driving. The Ultimate trim features leather seating surfaces, heated and ventilated front seats, heated rear seats, a heated steering wheel, power-adjustable drivers seat with memory, dual-zone automatic climate control, power sunroof, and proximity key with push-button start. Technology highlights include dual 10.25-inch displays with a digital instrument cluster and touchscreen infotainment system, built-in navigation, Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto, Bose® premium audio, wireless phone charging, Hyundai Bluelink®, and a rearview camera. Hyundai SmartSense safety technologies include Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Blind-Spot Collision-Avoidance Assist, Rear Cross-Traffic Collision-Avoidance Assist, Lane Keeping Assist, Lane Following Assist, Smart Cruise Control with Stop & Go, Highway Driving Assist, and Driver Attention Warning. The 2021 Hyundai Elantra Ultimate delivers an exceptional combination of premium comfort, advanced safety, modern technology, fuel efficiency, and everyday practicality, making it an outstanding choice for drivers seeking a fully equipped compact sedan.
