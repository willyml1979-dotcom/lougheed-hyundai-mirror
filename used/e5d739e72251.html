The 2025 Kia Seltos LX AWD is a stylish and versatile compact SUV that combines modern design, fuel-efficient performance, practical technology, and confident all-weather capability. With its spacious five-passenger interior and flexible cargo area, the Seltos is an excellent choice for commuting, family use, and weekend adventures. Powered by a 2.0L 4-cylinder engine producing 146 horsepower and 132 lb-ft of torque, paired with an Intelligent Variable Transmission (IVT), the Seltos delivers smooth and efficient everyday performance. Its All-Wheel Drive system provides added traction and stability in rain, snow, and changing road conditions. Inside, the Seltos LX AWD offers comfortable seating for five, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, air conditioning, cruise control, remote keyless entry, and 60/40 split-folding rear seats for additional cargo versatility. Technology highlights include an 8-inch touchscreen infotainment system with wireless Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, Bluetooth® connectivity, USB ports, steering wheel-mounted audio controls, and a rearview camera. Advanced safety technologies include Forward Collision-Avoidance Assist, Lane Keeping Assist, Lane Following Assist, Driver Attention Warning, High Beam Assist, and Electronic Stability Control. The 2025 Kia Seltos LX AWD delivers an excellent combination of modern styling, fuel efficiency, everyday comfort, advanced safety, and year-round capability, making it a practical and well-equipped compact SUV.
