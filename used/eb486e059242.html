The 2023 Honda Odyssey EX-L is a spacious and well-equipped family minivan that combines powerful V6 performance, premium comfort, advanced safety, and exceptional versatility. With its refined styling, roomy three-row interior, and family-friendly features, the Odyssey is an excellent choice for everyday transportation and long-distance travel. Powered by a 3.5L V6 engine producing 280 horsepower and 262 lb-ft of torque, paired with a smooth 10-speed automatic transmission, the Odyssey delivers responsive acceleration and comfortable highway performance. The EX-L trim offers premium features including leather-trimmed seating, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, power-adjustable front seats with driver memory, tri-zone automatic climate control, a power moonroof, power sliding doors, and a power tailgate. Honda’s Magic Slide® second-row seats provide added flexibility and convenient access to the third row. Technology highlights include an 8-inch touchscreen infotainment system with Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, Bluetooth® connectivity, multiple USB ports, steering wheel-mounted controls, and a multi-angle rearview camera. Honda Sensing® safety technologies include Collision Mitigation Braking System™, Adaptive Cruise Control, Lane Keeping Assist System, Road Departure Mitigation, and Traffic Sign Recognition. Blind Spot Information and Rear Cross Traffic Monitor provide additional confidence. The 2023 Honda Odyssey EX-L delivers an excellent combination of V6 performance, premium comfort, advanced safety, and family-friendly versatility, making it an outstanding choice for drivers seeking a well-equipped minivan.
